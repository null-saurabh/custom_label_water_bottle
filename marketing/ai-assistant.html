The purpose

Less scattered.
More considered.

Designed around the needs of teams handling customer enquiries and operational follow-up.

01 / Understand

Bring requirements together

The aim is to turn an enquiry into a clear draft of what is needed and what still needs confirmation.

02 / Organise

Prepare the next steps

Explore useful order drafts and production or delivery summaries, ready for a person to review.

03 / Follow through

Keep approval with people

Plan follow-up and payment reminder drafts that require human approval before sending.

Planned workflow — not yet integrated

A draft is a starting point.
Approval is a human step.

The intended flow keeps people responsible for order approval and customer communication. It is planned and not yet integrated.

  1. 01

    Customer enquiry

    Capture the request and requirements.

  2. 02

    Structured draft

    Organise details and questions to confirm.

  3. 03 · Approval gate

    Human review

    A person checks the draft before approval.

  4. 04

    Approved order

    Proceed only after a person approves.

  5. 05

    Production/delivery summary

    Prepare an operational summary for review.

  6. 06

    Reminder draft

    Prepare follow-up or payment reminder text.

  7. 07 · Approval gate

    Human approval

    A person approves the reminder before sending.
